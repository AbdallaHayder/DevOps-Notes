# IAM (Identity and Access Management)

**IAM** controls who can access AWS resources and what they're allowed to do.
---
- **Users** represent individual peaople or services, while **Groups** let you manage permissions for multiple users together.
- **Policies** are JSON-based permission rules that define which AWS actions and resources are allowed or denid.
- **MFA (Multi-Factor Authentication)** adds an extra security later by requiring a second verification method in addition to a password.
-**Access** provide temporary permissions to users or AWS services without giving them permanent credentials.
---
The main idea: IAM helps enforce **secure, controlled, and least-privilege access** across AWS.
