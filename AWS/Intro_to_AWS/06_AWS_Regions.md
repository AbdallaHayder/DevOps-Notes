# AWS Regions

An AWS Region is a geographic area that contains multiple AWS data centers and has a unique name such as `us-east-1` or `eu-west-2` which helps ensure redundancy high availibility for your application. Most AWS resources and services are region-specifc, so the region you choose affects where your data and infrastructure are hosted,

When choosing a region, consider four main factors:

- ### Compliance 
- Some laws or industries require data to stay in a specific country or region.

- ### Proximity to users
- choosing a region closer to your users reduces latency and imporves performance.

- ### Service availability
- not every AWS service or feature is available in every region.

- ### Pricing
- AWS costs can vary between regions

# 

### The goal is to choose a region that gives the best balance of `legal requirements`, `performance`, `available services`, and `cost`.
