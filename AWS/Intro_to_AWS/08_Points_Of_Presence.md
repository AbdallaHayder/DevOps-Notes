# AWS Points of Presence (Edge Locations)

AWS uses **Points of Presence (PoPs)**, including **Edge Locations and Regional Caches**, to deliver content closer to users around the world.

- Their main purpose is to **reduce latency** by serving cached content from a nearby location instead of a distant AWS Region.

- This is especially userful for **video streaming, gaming, websites, and other performance-sensitive applications**.

- Think of Edge Locations like **local warehouses for data**, allowing users to access content faster.

- AWS's global infrastructure map shows where its **Regions, Availability Zones, and Edge Locations** are located.
